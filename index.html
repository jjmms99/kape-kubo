Our Story

Rooted in Filipino Coffee Culture.

At Kape Kubo, we believe that great coffee brings people together. Inspired by Filipino hospitality and our country’s rich coffee-growing traditions, we created a place where every cup feels familiar yet exciting. We celebrate local flavors, support Filipino coffee culture, and welcome everyone to slow down and enjoy the little moments.

Kape Kubo is a made-up café, imagined as a neighborhood spot where students can study, friends can catch up, and a cup of barako sits next to a cup of ube.
